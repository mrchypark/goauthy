## Narrower open work

### [#65 — adopted contracts and focused implementation](https://github.com/mrchypark/goauthy/issues/65)

- Adopted policy: device approval atomically validates the approver's current authenticated browser session at the decision commit, independently of the session that initiated device authorization. Revocation committed before that fence prevents approval; a later revocation does not undo a committed decision. Issued delegated tokens survive actor logout until expiry, but a revoked actor cannot start a new exchange. If issuance has committed, an audit-write failure leaves issuance successful and is logged; mandatory audit delivery or replay is not required.
- The implementation is covered by focused regressions; [PR #131](https://github.com/mrchypark/goauthy/pull/131) records the independent review, Pro review, and required CI/merge evidence. PR #129 characterizes prior behavior and does not verify the adopted policies.
- Drop broad performance campaigns and speculative optimizations from this issue absent an adopted operating target or concrete performance need. Preserve the expiry report's limitations: the historical 45-case samples had a vacuous interaction-sentinel comparison and unaligned SQLite checkpoint history, and were not replaced. See [`expiry-backlog-65`](measurements/expiry-backlog-65/report.md).
